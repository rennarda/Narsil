burns bare skin
steals the breath
numbs the limbs
raises blisters within moments
stings the eyes and throat
eats through leather and rope
makes every step treacherous
draws blood from uncovered feet
leaves the mind reeling
calls scavengers from nearby passages
wakes vermin in the walls
makes every flame gutter blue
coats the throat in bitter dust
leaves the limbs weak and shaking
freezes exposed fingers
makes metal ring loud enough to carry
turns the air foul and close
sets old wounds throbbing
leaves a feverish rash
pulls at anything made of iron
