leans towards the same wall
returns from the wrong corner
arrives a breath late
grows warmer when watched
swallows every echo
ripples against the slope
fades whenever anyone speaks
moves when no one is looking
gathers where no wind blows
beats like a failing pulse
clings to anyone who enters
shivers at the sound of steel
settles only when the room is empty
tightens around the throat
creeps back after each footstep
seems to listen from beneath the floor
drifts uphill
pools around the living
holds its breath before a sound
trembles at a whispered name
leaves the skin cold and clammy
seems heavier at the centre
slides beneath the feet
appears to be listening
