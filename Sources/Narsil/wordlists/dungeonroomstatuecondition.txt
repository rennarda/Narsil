missing its head
split from crown to plinth
thick with soot
weeping black water
scored with knife marks
half-buried in rubble
wrapped in old cloth
painted with flaking red lines
stained with dry blood
covered in finger-sized scratches
leaning towards the wall
mottled with damp
chipped around the hands
wearing a crust of salt
crowned with cobwebs
slick with cold condensation
carved with fresh names
ringed by grey candles
blinded by hammered coins
smeared with black wax
hollow-eyed
broken at the waist
pitted by age
struck through by an iron spike
